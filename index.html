Bloom

A deep-V neckline, a rosette gathered at the waist, and a soft cascading drape through the skirt — three dresses built around a single feminine gesture. Rosé Bloom opened the house; Lime and Soleil carry its silhouette forward.

Muse

Shirt collar, four-button placket, flap chest pockets, a princess-seamed bodice with contrast topstitching, and a pointed handkerchief hem — tailored structure built for everyday elegance.

Fleur

A V-neck surplice bodice, ruched tasselled-drawstring sleeves, and a five-petal floral appliqué gathered at the waist above an A-line skirt — the house's most romantic silhouette, in three colourways.

Fleur Co-ord
